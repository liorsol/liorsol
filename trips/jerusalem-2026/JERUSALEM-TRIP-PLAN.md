## 5. 🌳 SATURDAY 20 June — Relaxed Day (City of David is closed)

Sleep in, slow breakfast and coffee at the caravans, kids playing. Then pick from the menu below — **no rush; check-out runs to ~21:30**, and you're driving **west on Route 1** at the end, so a stop on the way out is easy.

**Anchor for everyone: טיילת ארמון הנציב promenade walk** (next to camp, **free parking**, flat & little-kid friendly). Three connected stretches — **טיילת האז** (Haas, classic middle), **טיילת שרובר** (Sherover, west), **טיילת גולדמן** (Goldman, east through the ארמון הנציב grove). Big views, lawns, shade — perfect for a scavenger hunt / nature bingo. Always open, free.

### Then choose (all verified open on Saturday):

**🏛️ Option A — Old City morning (your pick) ✅**
The Old City is alive on Shabbat because the **Arab market is open**.
- Enter at **שער יפו (Jaffa Gate)** → **מגדל דוד (Tower of David museum)** — ✅ open **Sat 10:00–14:00**, excellent museum + ramparts/views, a great kid-friendly intro to Jerusalem's history.
- Walk down through the **שוק (Old City market)** — the lively lanes through the **Muslim/Christian Quarters** (spices, sweets, trinkets) → continue to the **כותל המערבי (Western Wall)**, always open & free.
- Go **in the morning** (cooler), keep it shorter for the 3–4-yo, bring **hats + water**.
- **Lunch back at התחנה הראשונה** (5–10 min, GF-friendly & open Shabbat — §6).
- **Parking:** **חניון קרתא** at שער יפו (closest to Tower of David/market). ⚠️ Confirm it's operating Saturday.

**🦌 Option B — עמק הצבאים (Gazelle Valley) ✅**
Israel's largest urban nature reserve — a free-roaming gazelle herd, easy trails, observation hides, lakes. **Free entry, open all daylight hours** incl. Saturday. ~15 min west. Wonderful, calm, kid-pleasing nature without a big drive. ⚠️ Limited parking at the entrance (גבעת מרדכי) — arrive earlier.

**🖼️ Option C — מוזיאון ישראל (Israel Museum) ✅**
World-class, ✅ open **Sat 10:00–17:00**. The **model of Second-Temple Jerusalem**, the **Shrine of the Book** (Dead Sea Scrolls), and a hands-on **Youth Wing** are all big hits with kids. On-site parking, דרך רופין 11. ~15 min. (Best if you want indoor/AC on a hot afternoon.)

**🔬 Option C2 — מוזיאון המדע ע"ש בלומפילד (Bloomfield Science Museum) ✅**
Hands-on, push-every-button science museum — a perfect rainy-or-roasting-day pick for ages ~5+. ✅ open **Sat 10:00–16:00** (often to 17:00 in summer). Right next to the Israel Museum at גבעת רם (שדרות המוזיאונים 3), so you could pair the two. On-site parking. ⚠️ Confirm summer Saturday hours (02-6544888).

**🌿 Option D — Stay local & easy**
Picnic + games on the promenade lawns or in יער השלום, plus a stroll on **פארק המסילה** (Train Track Park) — the old rail line turned into a wooden boardwalk running from התחנה הראשונה through בקעה/German Colony; flat, shaded, great for kids on foot/scooter. Always open, free. Cap it with ice-cream in **עמק רפאים** (lively on Shabbat).

### 🏁 On the way home (Route 1 west) — pick one, all are west of Jerusalem
Leave camp **early-to-mid afternoon** and break the drive with a great stop in the right direction:

- **🏰💦 גן לאומי עין חמד (Ein Hemed / "אקווה בלה") ✅** — minutes off Route 1 in the Judean hills. A shaded gem on **נחל כסלון**: channels and pools where **kids can wade in clean water**, lawns, picnic tables, and a **Crusader fortress**. ✅ open **Sat 08:00–17:00** (summer). National-park entry fee. *My top pick for a hot afternoon — water + shade + history.*
- **🎢 קיף צובא (Kif Tzuba), קיבוץ צובא ✅** — a family **amusement park for ages 1–14**: roller coasters, boat ride, ninja course, carousels, Ferris wheel, bumper cars, big inflatables, toddler zone. ~**09:00–16:00**, ~105₪ online (June). ✅ open Saturday — **⚠️ book online in advance & confirm the Saturday is open to the public** (02-5347952). *Best if the kids want pure fun over nature.* ⚠️ Ask whether summer water attractions are running.
- **🌳 פארק איילון–קנדה (Ayalon–Canada Park) ✅** — free, open **Sat 08:30–17:30**, family trail **"שביל אמת המים"** (1.5 km / 2.6 km loop), the **עין איילון** spring & lake, antiquities, big shaded picnic lawns, just past **לטרון**. *Free & easy.* *(Note: מיני ישראל nearby is **closed Shabbat** — skip it.)*

### 🌙 Saturday evening (optional, only if you linger past ~20:30)
Most night attractions are quiet on Motzaei Shabbat in June, and you'll likely be driving home. If you do stay: **התחנה הראשונה** is lively Saturday night (food + buzz), or grab a proper post-Shabbat dinner before the drive. For anything ticketed, check the city's live highlights board — ⚠️ events rotate, so verify it's actually on for **Sat 20 June**: <https://www.jerusalem.muni.il/he/experience/allevents/highlight/>

> **Suggested Saturday flow:** slow morning + promenade → **Old City + lunch at התחנה הראשונה** → drive west, stop at **עין חמד** (or קיף צובא / איילון–קנדה) in the afternoon → home. Swap in עמק הצבאים / מוזיאון ישראל / בלומפילד / פארק המסילה depending on energy and heat.

## 6. 🍽️ Food Guide — Gluten-Free First (you'll mostly eat out)

**Tell every server clearly:** *"יש לנו ילד/ה עם צליאק — אנחנו צריכים מנה ללא גלוטן, בלי חשש לזיהום צולב."* Israel is very celiac-aware; the places below have a real GF answer.

### The Shabbat reality (plan meals to these windows)
Most **kosher** places close **Friday ~15:00 → Saturday ~20:30**. Realistic eat-out windows: **Friday lunch**, **Friday night** (Shabbat-open spots only), **Saturday daytime** (Shabbat-open spots only), **Saturday night** (everything reopens).

| Place | Hebrew | Area | GF notes | Open Shabbat? |

| **Grand Cafe** | גרנד קפה | דרך בית לחם | GF bread on hand, GF breakfast bowl, great GF chocolate cake. | ⚠️ Confirm |
| **Adom** | אדום | התחנה הראשונה | Lively, **✅ open Shabbat & Friday night**, GF "Nemesis" cake; ask for GF mains. | ✅ Yes |

### More options, by situation

**🥗 Old City lunch (if you do the Old City morning, Option A):** instead of driving back, eat **inside the Old City** — the famous **חומוס אבו שוקרי** and **חומוס לינא (Lina)** in the **Muslim Quarter** are open Saturday and are hummus/salad places (**naturally GF except the pita** — bring a GF pita from גלולס). Hearty, cheap, and right on your market walk.

**🍳 Breakfast / brunch (German Colony – עמק רפאים, open Shabbat):** **קפית (Caffit)**, **גרנד קפה (Grand Café)** and the Emek Refaim cafés do big family breakfasts with GF bread on request — a nice slow Saturday-morning option a few minutes from camp.

**🥤 Quick / kid snacks (mostly GF-safe):** smoothie & shake bars like **רי-בר (Re:Bar)**, frozen yogurt, and gelato (**וניליה / קרמה**) — easy wins for picky eaters and the celiac child.

**🧺 Picnic for the parks (Saturday way-home):** עין חמד / איילון–קנדה / קיף צובא all have picnic areas. Pack a cooler: GF rolls + cold cuts/cheese, cut veg, fruit, GF crackers/snacks, yogurt. Buy GF items at **גלולס before Shabbat** (closed Saturday).

**🌙 Saturday-night dinner (after ~20:30 everything reopens):** great moment for the GF "holy grail" **בן עמי (Baka)** or a fuller meal out before the drive, since Friday-night and Saturday-day kosher options are limited.

**⚠️ Kosher vs. open-Shabbat — the practical rule:** the best dedicated-GF spots (בן עמי, מוזס/Moses, אגס ותפוח) are **kosher and closed Friday night → Saturday ~20:30**. The reliably-Shabbat-open ones are **First Station, German Colony cafés, and Old City (Arab) restaurants**. Plan accordingly, and **phone ahead the same day**.

### 🛒 GF supplies (buy before Shabbat — these are closed Saturday)
- **Gluless (גלולס)** — dedicated GF shop, hundreds of products. Branches at the **Oranim junction (צומת אורנים)** gas station and at **מחנה יהודה**. Get GF buns/pita, snacks, breakfast items, cookies for the מנגל & the road.
- **Bareket (ברקת)** — GF patisserie, יד חרוצים 14 — GF bread/pastries.

### 🔥 Friday-night מנגל / BBQ at the camp — ✅ allowed & celiac-safe
The campsite has **dedicated grill stations (עמדות להדלקת מנגלים / פינות מנגל ייעודיות)**, a **shared field kitchen**, shaded **KKL tables**, **power outlets**, and a small kitchenette with stove & fridge.
- **Naturally GF:** fresh/marinated meat & chicken (check the marinade has no soy-sauce/flour), kebab without breadcrumbs, veg, corn, foil potatoes, salads (no croutons), rice.
- **Bring GF buns/pita** (Gluless) for the celiac child; keep them physically separate.
- **Avoid cross-contamination:** grill GF portions **first** on clean foil; use a **separate plate, tongs & serving spoon**; keep regular bread/crumbs away from the GF food.
- **Easy GF dessert:** fruit, marshmallows, GF cookies, ice cream.
- Bring: charcoal + lighter (or check the shared grill), foil, matches, trash bags, cooler + ice.
